Regional Bank Digital Maturity Assessment

20 questions across five dimensions, calibrated for US regional and community banks. About 8 minutes.

How to answer: pick the option that describes what the bank does today, and what you could show an examiner, not what's on the roadmap.
  • Each answer maps to a stage from 1 (ad hoc) to 5 (optimizing). Each dimension score is the average of its four answers.
  • You'll see results once every question is answered. The results show your radar, your two lowest dimensions, and the score pattern they point to.
  • If you're scoring your own area, have someone outside it check you. Self-assessments usually come out about a stage high.
